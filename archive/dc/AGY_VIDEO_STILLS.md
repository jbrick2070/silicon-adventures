# Birthday video: the opening stills

You are painting eight stills for a short, funny film. Local video tools will animate them, so each must be a clear, well-composed opening frame. Read `archive/birthday-video/SCRIPT.md` for the story.

## Rules

- Work in `D:\silicon-adventures`, on the branch `feature/asset-parity`.
- Create files only in `archive/birthday-video/stills/`. Do not touch anything else. Do not commit, push, or switch branches. Play no audio. Leave ComfyUI and its GPU alone.
- Each still is 1280 x 720, in one rich, lit 3D style. Match `art/castle-3d/A-PPCT-10008.png` and `art/es-3d/inhabitants/ACHR-16393.png`.
- Use a muted dark yellow key light and a cool fill, with no orange glow.
- The only lettering allowed is what the table asks for, spelled exactly.
- Do not copy any film's characters, costumes, vehicles or logos, or any real person's face.

## The two characters

Keep these the same in every still.

- **Duncan:** match `art/castle-3d/A-PPCT-10008.png` and `A-PPCT-106.png`: young man, chestnut hair, cream tunic, brown belt, navy breeches, brown boots.
- **Elron:** the aged wizard. Base him on `archive/es/plates/AOBJ-23092.png`.
- **The time machine:** a beige 1986 all-in-one computer on four iron cart wheels, with cables and a brass lever.
