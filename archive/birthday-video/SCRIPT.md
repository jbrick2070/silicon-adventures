# Birthday video script

A surprise for 7 November 2026, about 90 seconds. The video never shows or names the person it is for. The two time travellers are the game's own people, so nothing here copies a film's characters or real actors.

**Cast:** Duncan from The Enchanted Castle, and Elron, the aged wizard of Enchanted Scepters. Their time machine is a beige 1986 Macintosh on wheels, with a flux of sparks where a floppy disk should go.

| Shot | Seconds | Picture | Words |
| --- | ---: | --- | --- |
| 1 | 8 | Night. A stone workshop, full of cables and candles. The Mac on wheels hums. | ELRON: We go back to 1986, to the night it was all made. |
| 2 | 6 | Duncan climbs on, nervous, holding a bag of rocks. | DUNCAN: Is this safe? |
| 3 | 6 | Elron turns a dial. The Mac's screen shows dates racing past. | ELRON: Perfectly. |
| 4 | 5 | The screen shows a sad face and a bomb icon. Sparks fly. | ERROR: Set clock to 1986 failed. |
| 5 | 7 | They vanish in a white flash, leaving two burning wheel tracks on the flagstones. | |
| 6 | 9 | They land in a neon city where everything is slightly pixelated. A giant sign reads Enchanted Scepters: now in real 3D. | DUNCAN: What year is this? |
| 7 | 6 | The sign flickers. The world dissolves into 1-bit dither. | ELRON: Oh dear. We are inside it. |
| 8 | 5 | Black and white floods with colour, and they fall into the Great Hall. | |
| 9 | 26 | Real gameplay: the marsh, the shark, the dragon, the ropes, the Black Knight on his throne, and a scepter turning in the air. | Game sound only. |
| 10 | 8 | The empty throne. | |
| 11 | 6 | Title card in the disk's font. | Happy Birthday. Some games never get old. Neither do you. |

**Sound:** the disks' own sounds and music. No film music.

**Build:** shots 1 to 8 are generated locally in ComfyUI from the painted game art. Shots 9 and 10 are recorded from the finished game, so they wait until the art is locked. All of it is cut together with ffmpeg.
